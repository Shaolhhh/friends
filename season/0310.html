[Scene: Central Perk, Chandler and Rachel are sitting on the couch.]

（看漫画）呃……我不知道。

什么？

瑞秋？

是的。

（对钱德勒）呃，你相信吗？

Chandler: (thinks about it) Yeah?
（想了想）是吗？

Opening Credits

（挥动球拍）还有潇洒的风度。 (does a backswing and hits Sarah who’s started up the stairs, knocking her down, they both watch in horror.)

好吧，再见。 (starts to leave)

Joey: And I gotta go sell some Christmas trees.

（对乔伊）嘿，你晚上睡得怎么样？

Phoebe: Really?
真的吗？

(Phoebe turns and looks at Monica, while Joey frantically motions to Chandler to help him out.)

Chandler: Yes.
是的。 Yes, and ah, ah, the trees are happy too, because for most of them, it’s the only chance to see New York.
是的，啊啊，树木也很高兴，因为对于它们中的大多数来说，这是参观纽约的唯一机会。

[cut to Gunther retraining Rachel.]

Gunther: ...and after you’ve delivered the drinks, you take the empty tray....
......送完饮料后，你拿走空托盘......

甘瑟，甘瑟，拜托，我在这里工作了两年半，我知道空托盘去那里。 (points to the counter.)

如果你把它们放在这里怎么办？ (sets the empty tray on another stack of empty trays on the back counter.)

Rachel: Huh.
呵呵。 Well, y'know that’s actually a really good idea, because that way they’ll be closer to the mugs.
嗯，你知道这实际上是一个非常好的主意，因为这样他们就会更接近杯子。 Y'know what, you should have the other waitresses do that too.

哎呀，我总是听到他们谈论这个，我只是一直以为那是他们去过的俱乐部。 Oh God, I’m, I’m sorry.
天哪，我，我很抱歉。 (walks away)

亲爱的。

[Scene: Sarah’s bedroom, her room is decorated with a space motif.]

Ross: So, this must be kinda neat for ya, huh?
那么，这对你来说一定很整洁，对吧？ I mean, your Dad tells me that you get a couple of days off school, and you, you ah, don’t have to sell those cookies anymore.
我的意思是，你爸爸告诉我你可以休几天假，你，你啊，不用再卖那些饼干了。

嗯，我有点想卖饼干。 The girl who sells the most wins a trip to Spacecamp, and gets to sit in a real space shuttle.
销量最高的女孩将赢得一次太空营之旅，并可以乘坐真正的航天飞机。

Ross: Wow, you ah, you really like all this space stuff, huh?
哇，你啊，你真的很喜欢这些太空的东西，是吧？

Sarah: Yeah.
是的。 My Dad says if I spend as much time helping him clean apartments, as I do daydreaming about outer space, he’d be able to afford a trip to the Taj Mahal.
我爸爸说，如果我花同样多的时间帮助他打扫公寓，就像我做外太空白日梦一样，他就能负担得起去泰姬陵的费用。

Ross: I think you would have to clean a whole lot of apartments to go all the way to India.
我想你必须打扫很多公寓才能一路去印度。

Sarah: No. The one in Atlantic City, Dad loves the slots.
不。在大西洋城，爸爸喜欢老虎机。 He says he’s gonna double the college money my Grandma left me.
他说他要把我奶奶留给我的大学学费翻倍。

Ross: Huh.
呵呵。 Well, good luck to Dad.
嗯，祝爸爸好运。 Say, how many more boxes would you have to sell in order to win?
比如说，你还需要卖出多少盒才能获胜？

去年获胜的女孩卖出了四百七十五件。

Ross: Yeah.
是的。

Sarah: So far, I’ve sold seventy-five.
到目前为止，我已经卖了七十五张。

Ross: Four hundred, huh?
四百吧？ Well, that sounds do-able.
嗯，这听起来可行。 (starts to get out his wallet) How much are the boxes?
（开始掏钱包）这些盒子多少钱？

Sarah: Five dollars a box.
五块钱一盒。

Ross: (puts away his wallet) And what is second prize?
（收起钱包）二等奖是什么？

Sarah: A ten speed bike.
十速自行车。 But, I’d rather have something my Dad couldn’t sell.
但是，我宁愿拥有一些我爸爸卖不掉的东西。

Ross: Well, that makes sense.
嗯，这是有道理的。

Sarah: Could you do me one favor, if it’s not too much trouble?
如果不太麻烦的话，你能帮我一件事吗？

Ross: Yeah, Sarah, anything.
是的，莎拉，什么都可以。

Sarah: Could you pull open the curtains for me?
你能帮我拉开窗帘吗？ The astronauts from the space shuttle are gonna be on the news, and since we don’t have a TV, the lady across the alley said she’d push hers up to a window, so I could watch it.
航天飞机上的宇航员将出现在新闻中，由于我们没有电视，巷子对面的那位女士说她会把电视推到窗户上，这样我就可以观看。

[Scene: A hallway, Ross is selling Brown Bird cookies for Sarah, he stops and knocks on a door.]

（透过她的猫眼，我们看到罗斯站在走廊里。） 是吗？

我要去。 I’m going.
我要去。 (goes across the hall to knock on another door.)

好的！！
