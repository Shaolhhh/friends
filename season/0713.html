[Scene: Joey and Rachel's, Rachel is trying to move Joey’s chair and not having much luck at it as Joey enters.]

Joey: Hey.
嘿。

Rachel: Hey.
嘿。

Joey: What are you doing?
你在干什么？

Rachel: Well, y’know I was thinking of moving the couch over here.
好吧，你知道我正在考虑把沙发搬到这里。

Joey: (laughs) Why would you want to do that?
（笑）你为什么要这么做？

Rachel: So that there will be a decent place for me to sit.
这样我就有一个像样的地方可以坐了。

Joey: Rach, there is a decent place to…
瑞秋，有一个不错的地方……

Rachel: And your lap does not count!
你的大腿可不算座位！ Okay?
好的？ Come on help me move this.
快来帮我搬一下这个吧。

Joey: No.
不。 No.
不。 No.
不。

Rachel: No?
不是了？

Joey: No.
不。 Rosita does not move.
罗西塔没有动。

Rachel: I’m sorry, Rosita?
对不起，罗西塔？ As in…
正如在...

Joey: As in Rosita does not move.
就像罗西塔不动一样。

Rachel: Joey, it’s just a chair!
乔伊，这只是一把椅子！ What’s the big deal?
有什么大不了的？

Joey: The big deal is that it is the exact equal distance from the bathroom to the kitchen and it’s at the perfect angle so you don’t get any glare coming of off Stevie.
最重要的是，浴室到厨房的距离完全相等，而且角度完美，所以你不会从史蒂夫身上看到任何眩光。

Rachel: Stevie the TV?
史蒂夫电视？

Joey: (glaring at her) Is there a problem?
（瞪着她）有什么问题吗？

Rachel: No!
不！ (Joey sets his beer and bag of chips down and heads into his room.) Oh what does he know!
（乔伊放下啤酒和一袋薯片，走进自己的房间。）哦，他知道什么！ Come on Rosita, us chichas got to stick together!
来吧，罗西塔，咱们姑娘要互相帮忙！ (She tries pulling on the back of the chair, until the hinge breaks and the back falls off.) You bitch!
（她试图拉动椅背，直到铰链断裂，椅背掉落。）你这个混蛋！

Opening Credits

[Scene: Central Perk, Chandler and Ross are on the couch talking. Phoebe is getting coffee.]

Ross: Hey, y’know what’s weird?
嘿，你知道有什么奇怪的吗？ After you guys get married, when you introduce me to people you’re gonna have to say, "This is my brother-in-law Ross."
等你们结婚以后，介绍我的时候就得说：“这是我大舅子罗斯。” Not, "My friend Ross," "brother-in-law Ross."
不能只说“朋友罗斯”，得说“大舅子罗斯”。 That’s weird isn’t it?
这很奇怪不是吗？

Chandler: Couldn’t I just say, "This is Ross?"
我不能直接说“这是罗斯吗？”

Ross: (disappointed) Sure, do whatever you want.
（失望）当然，你想做什么就做什么吧。

(Phoebe sits down between Chandler and Ross.)

Monica: (entering, carrying a newspaper) Hey Ross!
（拿着报纸进来）嘿罗斯！ So, I was checking out the uh, real estate section…
所以，我正在查看呃，房地产部分......

Ross: Yeah?
真的？

Monica: Look at this.
看看这个。 (Hands him the newspaper.)
（把报纸递给他。）

Ross: Oh, it looks like mom and dad’s house.
哦，看起来就像爸爸妈妈的房子。 Oh, it even has a tree with a broken limb out front and the uh, the window in the attic is…Oh my God!!
哦，它前面甚至有一棵树枝断了的树，呃，阁楼上的窗户是……哦天哪！

Phoebe: What?
什么？ What happened to the window in the attic?!
阁楼的窗户怎么了？！

Monica: I can’t believe mom and dad are selling the house!
我不敢相信爸爸妈妈要卖房子！

Ross: I can’t believe they-they didn’t even tell us!
我简直不敢相信他们——他们甚至没有告诉我们！

Phoebe: I can’t believe I still don’t know what happened to the window in the attic!
我不敢相信我仍然不知道阁楼的窗户发生了什么事！

(Ross calls his parents on his cell phone.)

Ross: (on phone) Uh, hello dad!
（电话中）呃，你好爸爸！ Monica and I just saw the house in the paper!
莫妮卡和我刚刚在报纸上看到了那栋房子！ (Listens) Yes we’re surprised!
（听）是的，我们很惊讶！ (Listens) Who did you leave a message with?
（听）你给谁留言了？

Chandler: (knocking on the window while outside) Sorry!
（在外面敲窗户）对不起！ (Runs off.)
（跑开。）

[Scene: Joey and Rachel's, Joey is examining the injury to Rosita while Rachel is apologizing to him.]

Rachel: Joey, Joey I am so sorry.
乔伊，乔伊，我很抱歉。

Joey: I told you not to move it!
我告诉过你别动它！ Rach, how would you feel if say, I wanted to move you mom, and you said don’t, and I did it anyway and her head fell off?
瑞秋，如果你说，我想搬走你，妈妈，你说不要，但我还是这么做了，她的头掉了下来，你会有什么感觉？

Rachel: Okay, come on—Joey, I’ll buy you a new one!
好吧，来吧——乔伊，我给你买一件新的！ All right?
好的？ We’ll go down to the store right now and we’ll-we’ll get you a new chair.
我们现在就去商店，我们会给你买一把新椅子。

Joey: (slowly turning and glaring at her) She’s not even cold yet!
（慢慢转身瞪着她）她还没冷呢！

Rachel: But don’t you think Rosita would’ve wanted you to move on?
但你不认为罗西塔会希望你继续前进吗？ I mean y’know, she did always put…your comfort first.
我的意思是你知道，她总是把……你的舒适放在第一位。

Joey: That’s true.
这是真的。

(Rachel turns for the door and makes the "Wow!" face.)

Rachel: (grabbing her coat) Okay?
（抓住她的外套）好吗？ You ready?
你准备好了吗？

Joey: Yeah, I…
是的，我…… (Shuts off the TV.) I don’t want Stevie to see her like this.
（关掉电视。）我不想让史蒂维看到她这个样子。

[Scene: Monica and Chandler's, Ross and Monica are still going on about the house.]

Ross: I can’t believe we have to say goodbye to the house we grew up in.
我不敢相信我们必须告别我们长大的房子。 Man, some-some stranger’s gonna be living in my room.
伙计，某个陌生人将住在我的房间里。

Monica: Well, after 15 years of mom and dad keeping it as a shrine to you, it’s time the velvet ropes came down.
嗯，爸爸妈妈把它当作你的圣地守护了 15 年，现在是时候放下天鹅绒绳索了。

Ross: They kept your room for a while.
他们保留了你的房间一段时间。

Monica: Oh please!
哦，拜托！ Dad turned my room into a gym 20 minutes after I moved out!
我搬出去20分钟后，爸爸就把我的房间变成了健身房！ I gotta say, a tanning bed and a stack of Victoria’s Secret catalogues, not a gym!
我得说，是一张日光浴床和一堆维多利亚的秘密目录，而不是健身房！

Ross: Come on, you know they love you.
来吧，你知道他们爱你。

Monica: As much as they love you?
像爱你那么爱我吗？

Ross: I was their first born!
我是他们的长子！ They thought she was barren!
他们以为她不能生育！ It’s not my fault.
这不是我的错。

Phoebe: (entering) Hey.
（进来）嘿。

Ross: Hey.
嘿。

Monica: Hey!
嘿！

Phoebe: Ugh, I hate this year!
唉，我讨厌今年！

Ross: What’s wrong with this year?
今年到底出了什么问题？

Phoebe: Well okay, it’s already February and I’ve only given two massages and they were both the worst tippers in the world!
好吧，已经是二月了，我只做过两次按摩，而他们都是世界上最不给小费的人！

Monica: That was me and Ross.
那是我和罗斯。

Phoebe: Oh that’s right!
哦，原来如此！

Ross: Hey, y’know if you want to pick up some extra cash?
嘿，你知道你想不想赚点额外的现金吗？ Some friends of mine made good money doing telemarketing.
我的一些朋友做电话营销赚了很多钱。

Monica: Oh that’s a great idea.
哦，这是个好主意。 You’re really good on the phone.
你的电话技术真的很好。

Phoebe: Yeah and yeah, and it would probably be better than the last telephone job I had.
是的，是的，这可能比我上次的电话工作更好。 Y’know, I probably wouldn’t have to say spank as much.
你知道，我可能不必说那么多打屁股的话。 (Monica and Ross are shocked.)
（莫妮卡和罗斯很震惊。）

Ross: What?
什么？

Phoebe: Oh yeah, like you never called!
哦，是的，就像你从未打电话过一样！

[Scene: The telemarketing office, Phoebe is getting shown to her desk by the supervisor.]

Supervisor: So basically this is very easy.
所以基本上这很容易。 You read from the script and try to sell as much toner as you possibly can.
您阅读剧本并尝试销售尽可能多的碳粉。

Phoebe: Okay, I can do that!
好吧，我能做到！ Oh, by the way, I love my office.
哦，顺便说一句，我喜欢我的办公室。

Supervisor: (laughs) Why don’t we do a trial run.
（笑）我们为什么不进行试运行呢？

Phoebe: Oh okay.
哦好的。 Umm, all right.
嗯，好吧。 (Picks up the phone and starts reading from the script.) Hi, this is Phoebe from Empire Office Supplies, can I speak to your supply manager please?
（拿起电话并开始阅读剧本。）嗨，我是帝国办公用品公司的菲比，我可以和你们的供应经理通话吗？

Supervisor: I’m the supply manager.
我是供应经理。

Phoebe: Umm, okay I would like to talk to you about your toner needs.
嗯，好吧，我想和您谈谈您的碳粉需求。

Supervisor: We don’t need any toner.
我们不需要任何碳粉。

Phoebe: Oh okay, well I’m sorry to bother you.
哦，好吧，抱歉打扰你了。 Bye-bye.
再见。 (Hangs up the phone.) Yeah you’re right, this is easy.
（挂断电话。） 是的，你是对的，这很容易。

Supervisor: Okay, what was wrong with that call?
好吧，那通电话有什么问题吗？

Phoebe: Oh well, all right…um, no offense, but you were kind of rude.
哦，好吧……嗯，无意冒犯，但你有点粗鲁。

Supervisor: They’re always going to tell you they don’t need toner, but that’s okay because whatever they say, you can find the answer to it here in this script.
他们总是会告诉您他们不需要碳粉，但这没关系，因为无论他们说什么，您都可以在此脚本中找到答案。

Phoebe: Oh.
哦。

Supervisor: So, I think you’re ready to sell toner, do you have any last questions?
所以，我认为您已经准备好销售碳粉了，您还有最后一个问题吗？

Phoebe: No.
不。 (Pause) Oh wait yes!
（停顿）哦等等，是的！ I do, I do have one question.
是的，我确实有一个问题。 What is toner?
什么是碳粉？

[Scene: Joey and Rachel's, Chandler is entering.]

Chandler: Joey!
乔伊！ Joe!
乔！ (Sees that he’s not here and starts investigating. He picks up the bag of chips.) Full bag.
（发现他不在，开始调查。他拿起那袋薯片。）满袋。 (He picks up the beer.) Beer’s still cold.
（他拿起啤酒。）啤酒还冷。 Something terrible must’ve happened here!
这里肯定发生了什么可怕的事情！ (He decides it’s not that important; sits down on Rosita, and the back falls off causing him to flip over.) Oh no-no-no-no-no-no!
（他觉得这没那么重要；坐在罗西塔身上，靠背掉了下来，导致他翻了个身。）哦不不不不不不不！ (Runs over to Stevie.) Stevie, I was never here!
（跑向史蒂维。）史蒂维，我从来没有在这里！ (Runs out.)
（跑出去。）

[Scene: Ross and Monica’s parent’s garage, Ross and Monica are arriving to go through their things. Mr. Geller is in the garage.]

爸爸？

我在这儿！

（和莫妮卡一起进来）嘿！

天哪，你们还出来看我工作，好像就在昨天。

爸爸，我们——我们不敢相信你要卖房子。

该让新的一家人在这里创造回忆了。但愿他们发现地基裂缝和天花板石棉之前，支票就已经兑现了。

Ross: (To Monica) Let’s grab our stuff and get the hell out of here.
（对莫妮卡）我们拿上东西离开这里吧。

很抱歉，我们无法再存储您童年的东西了。

哦，没关系，我迫不及待地想再次看到一切！ All of the memories…
所有的回忆……

好吧，我不知道下面的盒子里有什么，但我知道阁楼上有六七个简易烘烤炉。

Monica: I used to love to play restaurant.
我以前很喜欢玩餐厅。

是的，不像你以前那么喜欢玩生面糊吃者的游戏。

嘿，期望一个孩子等待灯泡来煮布朗尼蛋糕是不合理的！ (She goes to the attic.)
（她走向阁楼。）

Mr. Geller: So, I think you’re boxes are over here.
所以，我想你的盒子就在这里。 (They walk over to them.)
（他们走向他们。）

Ross: Wow!
哇！ Great!
伟大的！ (Finds a pack of cigarettes.) Wait, dad who-who’s cigarettes are these?
（找到一包香烟。）等等，爸爸，这些是谁、谁的香烟？

Mr. Geller: I don’t know.
我不知道。 They-they must be your mother’s, but please, please don’t ask her.
他们——他们一定是你母亲的，但是请，请不要问她。 I’ll throw these away.
我会把这些扔掉。 (He puts them in his pocket as Ross finds something of interest in one of his boxes.)
（当罗斯在他的一个盒子里发现一些有趣的东西时，他把它们放进了口袋。）

Ross: Cool!
太棒了！ Dad!
爸爸！ My report cards!
我的成绩单！ Hey, check this out dad, (reading his grades) Math, A.
嘿，看看这个爸爸，（读他的成绩）数学，A。 Science, A.
科学，A. History, A.
历史，A. Gym…
体育……(He puts it away and finds something else.) Oooh, my rock polisher!
（他把它收起来，又找到了别的东西。） 哦，我的岩石抛光机！

Mr. Geller: Oh look, look there’s your old makeup kit!
哦，看，看，这是你的旧化妆包！

Ross: It’s a clown kit!
这是小丑套装！ Clown kit!
小丑套装！

Mr. Geller: Well, the white seems to be untouched.
嗯，白色的好像没动过。 (He throws it back into the box as Mr. Geller moves a tarp and makes a discovery.) Uh-oh.
（他把它扔回盒子里，盖勒先生移动了一块防水布并有了一个发现。） 呃哦。

什么？

你打算怎么卖掉这个地方？

我想我不小心用了莫妮卡的盒子来让水远离保时捷。

哦不。 Dad!
爸爸！ Dad!
爸爸！ What…
什么…(He goes to open one of her boxes and it rips apart.) Oh God…everything’s ruined!
（他去打开她的一个盒子，它裂开了。）哦上帝……一切都毁了！ Dad, she’s gonna be crushed!
爸爸，她会被压垮的！

Ross: No!
不！

Mr. Geller: So it’s just your mother then.
所以那只是你的母亲。

呃，我要自杀了！ (There’s no response; no one even looks up.) I’ll get back to ya.

没有什么。

这不是同一件事！
